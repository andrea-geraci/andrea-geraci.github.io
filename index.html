About

I am Andrea Geraci. I am an Associate Professor at the Department of Economics and Management of the University of Pavia.

Before joining the University of Pavia, I worked as a research economist at the Competence Centre for Microeconomic Evaluation of the European Commission's Joint Research Centre in Ispra, Italy, between 2017 and 2022.

Between 2016 and 2017, I worked as Research Officer in the Employment, Equity, and Growth research team at the Institute for New Economic Thinking, University of Oxford.

I earned a PhD in Economics at the Institute for Social and Economic Research (ISER), University of Essex.

Research

  • Applied Microeconometrics
  • Policy Evaluation
  • Public Policy
  • Public Economics
  • Labour Economics

My research focuses on applied microeconometrics, with a specific interest in policy evaluation, public policy, public economics, labour economics, inequality, and social mobility.
